Good to know

How do I book?

Book online in a few taps. JC and Felicity take online bookings, and Kat, Teresa and Debby L book by phone at (207) 824-3452.

Is my online booking confirmed right away?

Not yet. You'll first get a text that your appointment is being reviewed. Once your stylist approves it, you'll get a second text saying it's accepted.

Are you open on weekends?

Yes, seven days a week, including Saturday until 7 and Sunday until 5.

Do you cut kids' hair and all hair types?

Yes. We're a family salon, so kids, grown-ups and every hair texture are welcome.

Is there a cancellation policy?

Some services may need a booking deposit, and late cancellations or no-shows may incur a fee. Please cancel as early as you can.
