FAQ

Good questions.

Don't see yours? Call or text (207) 601-1495.

Is the demo really free?

Yes. I build the demo before you pay anything, so you can see exactly what you'd be getting. If it's not for you, you don't owe anything.

What does the $500 cover?

Finishing your site with your changes and getting it live, with everything set up for you. It's one flat price, and I'll walk you through it before anything is final.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. Tell me about your business and I take care of the rest. If you can send a text, you can do this.

I already have a Facebook page. Do I still need a website?

A website works alongside it. It shows up when people search for you, it's easy to share, and it puts your hours, services and phone number in one clean place. Your site can link right to your Facebook page.

Where do the details on my site come from?

From you and from what's already public about your business. I never make up prices, menus or reviews. If something isn't known, it stays off the site until you tell me.

Can we talk it over first?

Of course. We can talk by phone, meet in person, or hop on Zoom, whatever's easiest for you.
