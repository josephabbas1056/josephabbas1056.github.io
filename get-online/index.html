Why a website

Right now, someone nearby is searching for what you do. Make sure they find you.

When people need a barber, a plumber or a place to eat, they pull out their phone and search. The businesses with a website show up and look ready. The ones without get skipped.

Look like the real deal

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is the demo really free?

Yes. I build it before you pay anything, so you see exactly what you'd be getting. If it's not for you, you don't owe a thing.

What does the $500 cover?

Finishing your site with your changes and getting it live, with everything set up for you. One flat price, and I'll walk you through it before anything is final.

What are the add-ons?

Extras beyond the standard site, like additional pages or a custom web address. Tell me what you have in mind and I'll quote it up front, before any work starts.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. Tell me about your business and I take care of the rest. If you can send a text, you can do this.

I already have a Facebook page. Do I still need a website?

A website works alongside it. It shows up when people search, it looks more established, and it puts your hours, services and phone number in one clean place. Your site can link right to your Facebook page.

Where do the details on my site come from?

From you and from what's already public about your business. I never make up prices, menus or reviews. If something isn't known, it stays off the site until you tell me.

Can we talk it over first?

Of course. Phone, in person or Zoom, whatever's easiest for you. Call or text (207) 601-1495.
