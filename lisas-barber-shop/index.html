Good to know

Do I need an appointment?

Nope. It's walk-ins only, first come, first served, Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 9 to 4.

Do you cut ladies' and kids' hair?

Yes. Men's, ladies' and kids' cuts. Kids get to pick a prize from the toy box when they're done.

What cuts do you do?

Flat tops, fades, regular cuts, mohawks, military cuts and more, plus eyebrow waxing.

How do I know if you're open today?

Check the open badge at the top of this page, or follow the shop on Facebook for any schedule changes. You can always call (207) 357-3824 first.

Is there parking?

Yes, there's plenty of parking outside and seating inside.
