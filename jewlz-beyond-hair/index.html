Questions

How do I book?

Call (207) 693-3899 and the team will find you a time with the right stylist or tech.

What are your hours?

Monday to Thursday 9am to 7pm, Friday 9am to 5pm, Saturday 9am to 2pm. Closed Sunday.

Do you do weddings?

Yes, in the salon or on-site at your venue. Bridal hair is $75 and makeup is $60. Bridal parties need a non-refundable deposit 90 days before the date.

How much is a haircut?

Shampoo, cut and style is $40, men's cuts are $25 and kids' cuts (under 12) are $20.

Do you do nails?

Yes: manicures from $30, pedicures from $35, gel, full sets, fills and nail art.
