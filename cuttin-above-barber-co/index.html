Before you swing byFAQ

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, walk-ins are always welcome. Appointments are encouraged so you can skip the wait.

How do I book?

Book online in a few taps, or call (207) 590-5340.

How much is a men's cut?

A regular men's cut is $30. On Tuesdays, regular men's cuts are $25.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes, kids 12 and under, including first haircuts. A standard kids' cut is $30, and designs start at $5.

Are there discounts?

Veterans, first responders and seniors (65+) get a standard cut for $23.
