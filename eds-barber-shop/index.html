Good to know

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome during open hours. Call ahead if you want to check the wait.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes. Kids' cuts are welcome, and the shop is known for being patient with little ones, including kids with sensory sensitivities.

Do you still do straight razor work?

Yes. Neck shaves and clean-ups are done with a straight razor, the classic way.

When are you open?

Tuesday 9am to 4pm, Thursday and Friday 9am to 5pm, Saturday 7:30am to 2pm. Closed Sunday, Monday and Wednesday.
