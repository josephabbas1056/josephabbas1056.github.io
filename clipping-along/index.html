Yes, all ages. We're patient with first haircuts and nervous little ones.

Do you do hot towel shaves?

Yes, hot towel shaves are by appointment.

When are you open?

Tuesday and Friday 9 to 5, Wednesday and Thursday 9 to 6, Saturday 9 to 1. Closed Sunday and Monday.
