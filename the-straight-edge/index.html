Good to know

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome, and you can also book a time online through Booksy so your chair is ready when you arrive.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes. Kids cuts are $15 for ages 1 to 4 and $20 for ages 5 to 17, with plenty of patience for wiggly little ones.

Can you come to me?

Yes. Susan will come to clients during off hours for an additional fee. Call to set it up.

Do you take cards?

Yes, credit cards are accepted. There's parking, and the shop is accessible and child-friendly.

When are you open?

Monday and Wednesday through Friday 9 to 6, Saturday 8 to 1. Closed Tuesday. Call ahead for Sunday.
