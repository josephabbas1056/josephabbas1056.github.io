Good to know

Can I walk in?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome and we'll fit you in when we can. For color, foils or perms, call ahead to book a time.

How do I book an appointment?

Give us a call at (207) 369-0260 during open hours: Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday 9 to 5, Saturday 9 to 1.

Do you cut men's hair and trim beards?

Absolutely. Barbering, buzz cuts and beard trims are all on the menu alongside women's cuts.

Do you do nails and tanning?

Yes. We offer nails and pedicures, and there's a tanning bed for quick sessions.

Is there parking?

Yes, free lot parking, and the shop is wheelchair accessible.
