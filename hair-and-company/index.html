Front page

A Main Street salon since 1985, settled into a bright new home.

Patty and Tammy have more than 80 years of experience between them, and their clients have followed them for decades, some for more than 25 years.

In January 2023 the salon moved from 248 Main Street, Norway to a freshly renovated storefront at 40 Main Street, South Paris, next door to H&R Block. Same stylists, same phone number, more windows.
