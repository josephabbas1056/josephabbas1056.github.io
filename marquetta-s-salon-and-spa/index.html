Good to know

How do I book?

Book online any time, or call 693-HAIR, that's (207) 693-4247. Appointments only.

Do you do hair extensions?

Yes. Marquetta has done extensions for over 15 years: bead bond, keratin glue bond, all types of tape-ins and sew-in wefts. A consult is recommended, and extensions are $85 per hour.

Do you do wedding hair?

Yes. A trial is $65 and an updo is $75. Contact the salon for travel details and the day-of deposit.

What are your hours?

Monday and Wednesday 9am to 7pm, Tuesday and Thursday 9am to 3pm, Friday 9am to 5pm. Closed weekends.
